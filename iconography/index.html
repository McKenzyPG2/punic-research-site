    Working method

    Keep observation and interpretation separate

    1. Photograph
    2. Vision-model observations
    3. Localized regions & proposed labels
    4. Catalogue comparison
    5. Human scholarly review pending
    6. Historical hypotheses not established

    Inventory IDs connect image regions to published inscription context. The vision model did not transcribe, translate or identify people. Catalogue comparisons here are assistant-assisted checks, not independent expert evaluation; catalogue interpretations are not infallible ground truth.

    With a larger, contextually controlled corpus, this representation could help test relationships between dedicatory formulae, motif combinations and monumental traditions. These three objects do not establish such relationships. Their date ranges overlap; they do not establish a chronological trend. Visual symbols do not prove donor ethnicity or personal religious identity.

    Provenance & limits

    Recorded run: 17 November 2026. Requested model alias: gpt-6.1-sol; an immutable model version was not supplied. The task requested visible geometry, approximate locations and cautious conventional labels. Photographs were supplied without catalogue descriptions; possible prior model familiarity cannot be excluded.

    Confidence is qualitative and uncalibrated. Bounding boxes are approximate, not validated segmentation. Five of nine conventional motif labels were withheld. Original Punic-letter readings remain unverified. Human scholarly adjudication is pending.

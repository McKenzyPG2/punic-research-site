What varies, and what follows?

All three retain Tyrian, merchants and shipowners. Koinon occurs both with and without Herakleistai; synodos occurs in the proclamation. Syntax and document function differ too. A simple cultic koinon / commercial synodos division does not follow.

One decree cannot establish diachronic identity change, separate associations, abandonment of a cult or every member’s ethnicity. Later lines are not a later historical phase.

Three explanations to investigate

Formulaic abbreviation

Does a later phrase shorten a designation already introduced?

Rhetorical context

Do enactment, honorific reasoning and proclamation use different conventions?

Affiliation emphasis

Could the retained expressions be significant in context? Omission alone cannot establish intention.

These hypotheses may overlap. They are not extractor findings or a demonstrated scholarly consensus.
